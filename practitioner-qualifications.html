Assessment Framework

Standard Assessment Framework for Chartered Professional Certifications

The Chartered Institute of Management Specialists (CIMS), USA awards its Chartered Professional Certifications through a structured Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) and professional assessment framework. The assessment is designed to evaluate an applicant's academic qualifications, professional competence, practical experience, and demonstrated expertise within their chosen discipline.

Assessment Structure

Three stages, one complete picture of competence

Every applicant is assessed across three weighted components, combining independent verification, real-world evidence and applied analysis.

20% Stage 1

Professional Credential “Third-Party” Verification

Applicants undergo verification of:

  • Academic qualifications
  • Professional certifications
  • Curriculum Vitae (CV) or Professional Biography
  • Professional experience
  • Identity verification documentation
Purpose: To establish the applicant's eligibility and verify the authenticity of all submitted credentials.
40% Stage 2

Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) Portfolio Assessment

Applicants demonstrate evidence of professional competence through the assessment of:

  • Professional experience
  • Leadership and managerial responsibilities
  • Industry achievements
  • Organisational contributions
  • Professional development
  • Evidence of applied knowledge within their discipline
Purpose: To evaluate knowledge, competence, and professional achievement acquired through practical experience.
40% Stage 3

Applied Professional Case Study

Applicants submit a structured case study describing a significant project, organisational challenge, strategic initiative, or professional assignment they personally managed or contributed to, demonstrating:

  • Problem identification
  • Professional analysis
  • Solutions implemented
  • Outcomes achieved
  • Lessons learned
  • Professional recommendations
Purpose: To assess the applicant's ability to apply professional knowledge in real-world practice.
Your Credential

A formally issued, internationally verifiable certificate

On successful completion of all three assessment stages and approval by the Governing Council, your Practitioner designation is conferred with a Chartered Professional Certificate bearing the Institute's seal, your unique credential reference, and a verification record held on file with CIMS.

  • Digitally verifiable through the Certificate Verification portal
  • Carries the post-nominal letters of your awarded designation
  • Recognised internationally across 170+ countries
  • Remains valid and in good standing under current Institute policy

Step by Step

The assessment process

From preliminary review to conferment, here's exactly what happens once you apply.

Preliminary application review.

Submission of supporting documentation.

Payment of the Professional Credential Verification & Assessment Fee.

Non-refundable

Independent verification of academic and professional credentials.

Completion of the Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL) Assessment Questionnaire.

Submission of the Applied Professional Case Study.

Independent assessment by the Institute's Assessment Panel.

Recommendation to the Governing Council for approval.

Upon approval and payment of the applicable Certification Fee, the Chartered designation is conferred.

Governing Council Communiqué

Enhancements to the Professional Recognition Framework

The Governing Council of the Chartered Institute of Management Specialists (CIMS), USA has approved enhancements to the Institute's Professional Recognition Framework to further strengthen the quality, clarity, and international standing of its professional qualifications and membership pathways.

These enhancements reinforce the Institute's commitment to competency-based professional assessment, Recognition of Prior Learning (RPL), independent evaluation, and internationally verifiable digital credentials.

All qualifications, memberships, and professional recognitions previously conferred by the Institute remain valid, retain their full standing, and continue to be recognised in accordance with the policies in force at the time of conferment. Applicants are encouraged to review the updated qualification pathways, which reflect the Institute's ongoing commitment to professional excellence, governance, and internationally recognised professional standards.

Effective Date: 7 August 2026 Approved by Resolution of the Governing Council
