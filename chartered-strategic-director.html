The Designation

What Is Chartered Strategic Director (CSD™)?

The Chartered Strategic Director designation is an executive recognition awarded through a rigorous professional assessment process rather than classroom attendance.

The designation recognises demonstrated achievement in:

  • Strategic Leadership
  • Corporate Governance
  • Executive Decision Making
  • Organisational Transformation
  • Business Growth
  • Leadership Impact
Why It Matters

Why Executives Pursue the Chartered Strategic Director (CSD™)

The Chartered Strategic Director (CSD™) designation is more than a professional title—it is a formal recognition of executive leadership, strategic influence, and organisational achievement.

Awarded by the Chartered Institute of Management Specialists (CIMS), USA, the designation acknowledges accomplished leaders who have demonstrated excellence in strategic management, governance, executive decision-making, and organisational transformation.

Successful candidates benefit from international executive recognition designed to strengthen their professional standing and distinguish them among senior leaders.

Executive Recognition of Career Achievement

How It's Awarded

A Recognition Earned Through Professional Assessment

The Chartered Strategic Director (CSD™) is awarded through a structured professional assessment process.

Candidates demonstrate executive competence through credential verification, leadership portfolio assessment, strategic case study submission, and executive endorsement.

This ensures that the designation reflects demonstrated executive achievement rather than classroom participation alone.

The Distinction

Why Distinguished Leaders Choose CSD™

Executives pursue the Chartered Strategic Director designation because it provides:

  • Independent recognition of executive leadership
  • Professional distinction among senior leaders
  • Enhanced executive credibility
  • Recognition of governance and strategic management expertise
  • Validation of organisational leadership achievements
  • Strengthened executive profile for board and advisory opportunities
  • An internationally awarded executive designation from the Chartered Institute of Management Specialists (CIMS), USA

Frequently Asked Questions

No. The designation is awarded through professional assessment and recognition of executive achievement.
Do I attend classes?
No.
Is there an examination?
No traditional examination. Candidates are assessed through documentation, case study submission, and professional review.
Can public sector leaders apply?
Yes.
Can entrepreneurs apply?
Yes.
Is the designation internationally recognised?
The designation is awarded by the Chartered Institute of Management Specialists (CIMS), USA and is intended to recognise distinguished executive leadership and strategic management achievement.
