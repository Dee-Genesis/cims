Digital Credentials & Verification

CIMS uses modern digital credential technology to improve the security, portability and independent verification of eligible professional awards.

Secure Digital Credentials

Depending upon the credential issued, CIMS digital certificates and badges may incorporate:

Unique Credential ID

Each eligible credential carries a unique identification reference.

QR Code Verification

QR-enabled credentials provide convenient access to the relevant verification record.

Blockchain-Secured Authentication

Eligible digital credentials incorporate blockchain-supported security features designed to strengthen credential integrity.

Online Credential Verification

Employers, institutions and other authorised parties can verify eligible credentials electronically.

Secure Digital Certificate and Badge

Successful recipients may receive a digital certificate and professional badge suitable for electronic professional use.

Professional Sharing

Digital credentials can be shared across LinkedIn and other appropriate professional platforms.

Protecting Credential Integrity

A digital credential should always be considered together with its corresponding verification record.

Please Note

Scope of CIMS Verification

CIMS reserves the right to investigate suspected alteration, falsification or unauthorised reproduction of its certificates, badges and professional designations.

CIMS verifies professional executive memberships, assessments and continuing professional training awards. CIMS does not provide academic degree verification or state statutory licensing verification.
