Overview

About the CTDMS™ designation

The Chartered Training and Development Management Specialist (CTDMS™) is a specialist-level credential from the Chartered Institute of Management Specialists (CIMS) that recognises individuals with exceptional expertise and leadership in the field of training and development management. It signifies a commitment to excellence in designing, implementing and evaluating training programmes to enhance organisational performance and employee development.

Holders of the designation are recognised as skilled training professionals who can translate learning needs into effective programmes — from training needs analysis through to evaluation of training effectiveness — while upholding the ethical and professional standards the discipline demands.

Entry requirements

To qualify for the CTDMS™ designation, candidates must meet the following criteria:

Academic background

A bachelor's degree or advanced diploma in human resource management, organisational development, education, or a related field from a recognised institution.

Professional experience

A minimum of 5–10 years of relevant experience in training and development management roles, encompassing training needs analysis, curriculum development, training delivery, and evaluation of training effectiveness.

Mode of assessment

Candidates' knowledge, skills and competencies are evaluated through a comprehensive assessment process, which may include:

  1. Review of academic qualifications, focusing on coursework related to training and development, adult learning theory, and instructional design.
  2. Documentation of professional experience, including training and development projects undertaken, accomplishments in improving employee performance, and impacts on organisational effectiveness.
  3. Examination of technical knowledge and competency in training methodologies, instructional design principles, learning management systems, and training evaluation techniques.
  4. Case studies or practical assignments assessing the candidate's ability to analyse training needs, design effective training programmes, deliver engaging training sessions, and measure training outcomes.

Standards & competencies

The CTDMS™ designation is awarded to individuals who demonstrate excellence in the following areas:

Training needs analysis & assessment
Curriculum development & instructional design
Training delivery & facilitation
Learning technologies & e-learning solutions
Training evaluation & ROI analysis
Employee engagement & development
Talent management & succession planning

Features & benefits

01

Professional recognition

Be recognised as a highly skilled and effective training professional, enhancing your credibility and reputation within the human resources community and among organisational stakeholders.

02

Career advancement

Position yourself for leadership roles in training departments, corporate universities, consulting firms, and educational institutions, with opportunities for career progression and increased responsibility.

03

Strategic impact

Play a crucial role in driving organisational success by developing the skills and competencies of employees, improving performance, and fostering a culture of continuous learning and development.

04

Continuous professional development

Commit to ongoing learning, staying updated on emerging trends, technologies and best practice in training and development to remain at the forefront of your field.
